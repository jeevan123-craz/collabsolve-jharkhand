🎯 Top 4 Jury Q&A Defense Sheet (Prepare These!) High Priority
Q1: "How does your system handle tribal languages like Santhali, Mundari, or regional dialects like Khortha?"
Answer: "Our audio pipeline routes acoustic input through multilingual speech models and passes the phonetic text to Gemini 2.5 Flash with few-shot Jharkhand dialect mappings. The AI translates regional idioms into standardized Hindi and English administrative records while preserving the exact ground context."
Q2: "What prevents malicious users from flooding fake issues to game the system?"
Answer: "We employ three layers of verification: First, semantic duplicate detection (/api/ai/check-duplicate) identifies coordinated bot spam. Second, GPS reverse geocoding verifies spatial clustering. Third, sentiment and urgency scoring flags inconsistent or fabricated descriptions for mandatory manual review."
Q3: "How scalable is the Twilio phone calling feature for thousands of users?"
Answer: "The telephony engine is decoupled into serverless webhook queues. Urgent alerts (e.g., public health hazards) are triggered instantly via priority queues, while routine resolution notifications are throttled during off-peak hours to respect telecom rate limits and optimize operational costs."
Q4: "Is this platform actually built, or is this just a mockup?"
Answer: "It is completely functional and production-ready. All 43 routes pass next build with zero TypeScript errors. The entire repository is published on GitHub at github.com/jeevan123-craz/collabsolve-jharkhand with Supabase realtime connectivity and Gemini API integration."
